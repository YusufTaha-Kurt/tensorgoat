# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TensorGoat to be a welcoming place to learn, for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation. This project is aimed at beginners; nobody should feel
embarrassed for asking a basic question here.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Explain, do not belittle.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and own your mistakes when you make them.
- Keep spoilers out of issue titles and public discussions; link to the
  walkthrough instead.
- Keep the project's purpose in mind: teaching people to defend ML systems.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political
  attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Asking for, or offering, help with attacking systems without authorisation.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of
conduct. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository
owner's GitHub profile, or use GitHub's "Report content" function on the
offending comment. All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.
